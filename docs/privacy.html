PhonicsQuest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

PhonicsQuest is an educational English-learning app for children. We have designed it to be safe for young learners. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not handle.

The short version

PhonicsQuest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics tracking of individuals.

What is stored, and where

To remember a learner's progress (stars, levels, mastery, and the profile name and avatar you choose), the app saves data only on your own device using the browser's local storage (localStorage). This information:

  • never leaves the device;
  • is not sent to any server we control;
  • can be cleared at any time by clearing the app/browser data.

A profile name is whatever you choose to type — we recommend a first name or nickname, not full names or other identifying details.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses are sent to us.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no behavioural or personalised advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

PhonicsQuest is intended for children and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no parental consent mechanism is required for data collection. We encourage parents and teachers to supervise young learners.

Third-party content

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts to display text. This may involve a standard web request to Google's font servers. No PhonicsQuest user data is shared in that request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
