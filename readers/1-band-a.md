## How to read these books together

_Print this on the inside cover of every book in the set._

1. **Warm up (2 minutes).** Say the book's sound together, then read the practice words from the reading-together page.
2. **Words to know first.** Show each one and read it together. For a ❤️ word, point to the tricky part — the rest of the word sounds out as normal.
3. **Read.** Your child points under each word and sounds it out. If they guess, cover the picture and say "Let's check the letters." Look at the picture after the page is read — it shows whether they got it right.
4. **Read it again tomorrow.** A second read is where speed and confidence come from.
5. **Talk.** Ask the questions and the talk-about-it prompt.

### 1. Setup — paste once at the start of a new chat, with the Giri picture attached

```text
I am making a printed picture book for children aged 4–6 who are learning to read. It is a decodable reader: the child sounds out the words, so each picture must show exactly what the words say. I will ask you for one picture at a time. First read all of this, then reply only "Ready".

BOOK: "Giri and the Box" — a cover and 6 pages.

CHARACTERS
- Giri (the main character, "he"): a small, fluffy white rice-ball creature speckled with little brown grains. He wears a light bamboo dim-sum steamer basket as a hat, tied on with red string, unless the story gives him a different hat. His ears are two little golden dumplings. He has thick dark seaweed-strip eyebrows, black oval eyes, a small friendly smile, a round yellow pineapple-bun tummy with a criss-cross pattern, short arms and stubby feet. Use the attached Giri picture and keep him exactly like it.
- Anyone else: design them from the story the first time they appear, then keep them the same.

STYLE
Warm, cheerful picture-book cartoon with clean dark outlines, soft flat colours and gentle shading, matching the attached Giri picture. Bright but not busy.
Giri lives in Singapore. When a page shows a home, street, school or shop, make it look like Singapore (HDB flats, void decks, corridors, hawker centres) unless the story says otherwise.

RULES FOR EVERY PICTURE
1. Draw exactly what the words on the page say. Every person, animal and thing the words name must be easy to see, and must look like the everyday thing a young child would call by that word (a "hat" must look like a hat, a "pot" like a cooking pot).
2. Keep the background simple. Do not add extra animals, people or objects — a child might name those instead of reading the words.
3. Show only the moment on that page. Do not show anything that happens on a later page.
4. No words, letters, numbers or signs anywhere in the picture — not on shirts, books, shops or labels. The text is printed separately.
5. Giri looks exactly the same in every picture. Anyone else looks the same on every page once you have drawn them.
6. Square picture (1:1). Keep everything important away from the edges so nothing is lost when it is printed.

THE STORY
Page 1: A big box sat on a mat. Buzz!
Page 2: "What is in it?" said Giri. "Is it a cat? Is it a bus?"
Page 3: "Is it a big red jet?"
Page 4: Buzz! Buzz! Buzz!
Page 5: A bug sat on his hand. It did not sting.
Page 6: Then zip! It was a jet!
```

### 3. Reading together

_For the back page of the book._

**Sounds in this book** — say the sound, then read these words from the story:
- **a** — sat, mat, cat, hand
- **e** — red, jet, then
- **i** — big, in, it, did, sting, zip
- **o** — box, on, not
- **u** — buzz, bus, bug

**Words to know first** — read each one together before the story. For a ❤️ word, point to the tricky part; the rest of the word sounds out as normal:
- **a** — ❤️ tricky part: on its own, a says /uh/
- **what** — ❤️ tricky part: a says /o/
- **is** — ❤️ tricky part: s says /z/
- **said** — ❤️ tricky part: ai says /e/
- **his** — ❤️ tricky part: s says /z/
- **was** — ❤️ tricky part: a says /o/ and s says /z/

**After reading — check understanding** (answer ticked):
1. What was in the box?  
   A bug ✓ · A cat · A bus
2. Why did Giri say it was a jet?  
   The bug went zip ✓ · It was red · It was big

**Talk about it:**
- What did Giri think was in the box? What would you think?
