## How to read these books together

_Print this on the inside cover of every book in the set._

1. **Before reading.** Go through the word meanings and the words to know first.
2. **Take turns.** Your child reads a page aloud; you read the next. Pause at the end of each page: "What just happened?"
3. **Tricky word?** Sound it out together rather than giving it straight away. If the code is new, say the word and move on.
4. **Read a favourite page again** with expression — the voices, the pauses.
5. **Talk.** Ask the questions and the talk-about-it prompts.
