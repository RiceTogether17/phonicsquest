## Full description (≤4000 chars)
```
PhonicsQuest turns early reading and primary English into a joyful adventure. Built for
children from their first sounds all the way to PSLE-style practice, it grows with your
learner through two clear pathways in one app.

EARLY READING QUEST (for new readers)
• Blending — push sounds together to read whole words
• Segmenting — break words back into their sounds
• Letter–sound recognition and sight words
• Gentle, encouraging feedback and a friendly owl mascot, Ollie

PRIMARY ENGLISH QUEST (for primary school)
• Grammar, vocabulary, and cloze passages
• Editing and writing practice
• An Exam Practice Hub with PSLE-style questions

WHY PARENTS AND TEACHERS LOVE IT
• Designed for kids — bright, simple, and age-appropriate
• Tracks progress, stars, and mastery so children see how far they've come
• Multiple learner profiles on one device
• Accessible: large touch targets, clear narration, and keyboard support
• No ads, no sign-up, no data collection — progress stays on the device

SAFE AND PRIVATE
PhonicsQuest collects no personal information. There are no accounts to create and nothing
is uploaded — your child's progress is saved only on your device.

Whether your child is sounding out their very first word or polishing exam technique,
PhonicsQuest makes practice something they look forward to. Start the quest today!
```
